## Findings

- **Subscription vCPU quota, not the module, failed the first phase-1
  apply.** The playground subscription had a regional `cores` limit of 10 and
  `standardDSv5Family` of 10 in every region checked. Two system plus two
  user `Standard_D2s_v5` nodes consume 8, so the cluster autoscaler could not
  add the node the two main pods needed, and `helm_release.n8n` hit its
  600 s timeout and rolled back (`atomic`). `az quota update` to 30 cores /
  20 DSv5 was approved immediately; the retry applied clean once the
  autoscaler's backoff from the earlier failure expired (about 12 min). The
  preflight script cannot see this because it checks SKU availability, not
  quota headroom; `tests/scripts/preflight-region-check.sh` gaining an
  `az vm list-usage` headroom check is a reasonable follow-up.
- The 1.13.0 upgrade rolled main, worker, and webhook pods once (new pod
  templates), with the expected brief worker capacity dip and no failed
  pods.

## Teardown

`terraform destroy` from `examples/small`; the override file was removed
afterwards. The example-owned zone `n8n.cmifro.com` was recreated for this
run, so its delegation at the registrar was not verified; the smoke test's
`--resolve` fallback was not needed because public resolution worked.
